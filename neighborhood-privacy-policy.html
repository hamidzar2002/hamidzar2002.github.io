Privacy Policy

The Neighborhood
Effective and last updated: 10 November 2026

The Neighborhood (Android package com.hamidzar2002.neighborhood) is provided by Hamid Zarrazvand, trading as hgamez. Contact: hamidzar2002@gmail.com.

No game account is required. Case progress is saved on your device. Optional usage analytics and game news are off by default. Optional rewarded ads and Google Play purchases use Google's services and can involve data leaving your device.

1. Game data on your device

The app stores completed cases, stars, collected records, experiment history, hypotheses, hints, broad age/privacy choice, preferences and unlocked-case receipts in local app storage. We do not ask for a date of birth. The game does not upload your raw dialogue or hypothesis text to an analytics service. Android may back up app preferences or transfer them to another device according to your system settings.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

The first five distinct cases are free and can be replayed. You can choose to load and watch a Google AdMob rewarded interstitial to unlock one next locked case. Entering the unlock-options page can start loading an ad before you choose to watch. The introduction explains the reward and offers Skip before playback. You may instead return to unlocked cases or purchase an unlock pack.

When advertising services are used, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share device/account identifiers, including the advertising ID where available, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses these for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. The app does not request GPS or precise-location permission. Consent and advertising privacy choices are presented where required and available through Google's User Messaging Platform; a privacy-choices action appears in Settings when required. Refusing personalised advertising does not necessarily stop all ad-related processing.

3. Optional analytics

Usage analytics is off by default. Users choosing age 16–17 or 18 or over may enable it in Settings when Firebase is configured. Firebase Analytics can then process app-instance/device information, app usage and numeric case/chapter actions such as experiment and hint counts, session timing and navigation. These measurements help improve the game. The app disables Firebase Analytics advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals. Turning analytics off stops future app analytics collection; it does not automatically erase information already processed by Google.

4. Optional game news

Game news is off by default. Eligible users may enable it in Settings and grant Android notification permission. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a registration token and installation/service information to deliver messages. Turning game news off disables messaging auto-initialisation and requests deletion of the current registration token. You can also disable notifications in Android settings.

5. Purchases

Google Play handles checkout and payment details. We do not receive your full card number or banking credentials. The app receives purchase product IDs, purchase tokens, purchase state and verification information to grant access, prevent duplicate grants and restore eligible purchases. Receipts and unlock state are saved locally. Google processes transactions under its own terms and privacy policy. The 10/25-case packs are consumed purchases; clearing app data or uninstalling can lose their local unlock record. Android backup is dependent on your settings. The all-100 purchase has a Google Play restore path.

6. Age and privacy choices

The app stores only your selected broad age band or Prefer not to say. Optional analytics and game news are disabled for Under 16 and Prefer not to say. Those choices apply child/under-age advertising protections and the app requests a general-audience ad-content limit. Age 16–17 applies teen advertising treatment. These controls do not mean advertising services process no data.

7. Recipients, retention and security

Google provides AdMob, consent, Firebase and Play Billing services. Service data may be processed on infrastructure outside your country under the provider's applicable safeguards and policies. Local progress remains until removed through app/system storage controls, subject to backups. Third-party retention follows the relevant service configuration and Google's policies; this policy does not promise a fixed deletion period for data held by Google. Network services use encrypted connections. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure.

8. Your choices and requests

You can change age/privacy choices, turn optional analytics/news off, use advertising privacy choices when offered, and clear local game data through Android app settings. Clearing data can also remove purchased pack access and progress; read the purchase limitations above first. Manage Android backups and advertising-ID controls in your device settings and Google Play transaction records through Google. For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact hamidzar2002@gmail.com. Tell us which app and service your request concerns; do not send card details. We will explain what we can identify and act on, including where Google must handle a request. Applicable data-protection rights and the right to complain to your competent supervisory authority remain available.

9. Support and policy updates

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, to answer your request and handle support records. We may update this policy as the app or its services change and will update the date above. Material changes will be communicated where required.
