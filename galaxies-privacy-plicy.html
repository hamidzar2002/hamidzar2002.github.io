Privacy Policy

Galaxies Logic Puzzle Lite
Last updated: July 23, 2026

Galaxies Logic Puzzle Lite is a free, ad-supported puzzle game with unlimited puzzles. It does not require an account. Game progress remains on your device, while Google AdMob may process limited device and usage information to provide, measure, and protect advertising.

Information We Collect

The developer does not directly collect identifying account information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or account details.

Local Game Data

The app stores limited gameplay information locally, including:

  • Your latest unfinished puzzle and drawn borders.
  • Local statistics such as completed puzzles, streaks, and hint count.
  • The completed-game count used to schedule occasional interstitial ads.
  • Whether onboarding and help information have been displayed.

The developer does not receive or sell this local gameplay information. If Android backup is enabled, Google may back up and restore it under your device and account settings.

Advertising And Information Processed By Google

The app uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display occasional interstitial advertisements. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and Google's configuration, Google may automatically collect or receive an IP address, approximate location derived from it, app and ad interactions, diagnostic or performance information, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID.

Google may use this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The developer may receive aggregated advertising reports but does not use them to identify individual players. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent And Advertising Choices

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices before requesting ads. Ads may be personalised, non-personalised, or limited according to your choices, location, and applicable law. When required for your region, the in-app Help page provides a Privacy Choices option for reviewing available choices.

Network Access

Puzzle gameplay works offline. Internet and network-state access are used for consent information and advertising. If an ad is unavailable, gameplay continues normally.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information. Advertising information described above is transmitted to or processed by Google to provide the advertising service. The developer does not transmit your local puzzle progress or game statistics to Google.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience logic puzzle game. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored gameplay data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. If Android backup restores the data, you may also need to manage the backup through your Google account or device settings. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Changes To This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. The current version will remain available at this address.
