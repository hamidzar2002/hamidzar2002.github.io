Privacy Policy

Galaxies Logic Puzzle
Last updated: May 25, 2026

Galaxies Logic Puzzle is a one-time paid puzzle game. The app does not show ads, does not use in-app purchases, does not require an account, and does not collect personal data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from users of Galaxies Logic Puzzle. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or account details.

Local Game Data

The app stores limited gameplay information locally on your device so the game can work properly. This may include:

  • Whether the first-launch help screen has been shown.
  • Your latest unfinished puzzle and drawn borders.
  • Local game statistics such as streak, completed puzzle identifiers, and hint count.

This data stays on your device. We do not receive it, upload it, sell it, or share it.

Ads, Tracking, And Analytics

Galaxies Logic Puzzle does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or third-party marketing tools.

Network Access

The app is designed to work offline and does not contact external servers for gameplay.

Data Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties because the app does not collect user data.

Children's Privacy

The app is a general-audience logic puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Data Deletion

Because gameplay data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app changes. If future versions introduce any data collection, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support contact listed on the app store page.
